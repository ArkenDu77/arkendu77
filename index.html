The Quiet Machine

Slow, calm documentaries about the hidden science and strange history of everyday things, made to fall asleep to.

The Quiet Machine is a YouTube channel. Each episode follows one ordinary object from your home, such as the refrigerator humming in the night, into its quiet mysteries: the science you cannot see, the people who shaped it, and the hidden systems that keep it working while you sleep. Episodes are long, gently narrated and illustrated, with no loud music and nothing to remember.

About this application

"The Quiet Machine" is also the name of the private software the channel owner uses to run the channel. It connects to Google only to manage the owner's own YouTube channel:

  • uploading the channel's own videos, captions and thumbnails, and organising them into the channel's playlists;
  • reading the channel's own statistics (views, watch time, traffic sources, audience retention) to improve future episodes.

It is not offered to the public. No other person signs in to it, and it does not access anyone else's account or data.
